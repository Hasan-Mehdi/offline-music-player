# H2 after a day of use: what changed (2026-10-15)

From the first full day with the Bluetooth build: the list of complaints, what the logs
said, and what's on the card now (firmware `rockbox.erosq` 56313FEC, Bluetooth patches
0017 and 0018, see [BLUETOOTH.md](BLUETOOTH.md)). Nothing here has run on the H2 yet.
Logs from the day: `data/h2_device/2026-10-15_day/`; the card before: `2026-10-15_pre_fixes/`.

## Buttons and the queue

| what | how |
|---|---|
| **Dislike and skip** | Hold Next for about half a second and let go, from any screen, lit or dark. "Let go: dislike and skip" shows when it's armed. Turning the wheel while holding Next is still brightness. |
| **Next at the end of the queue** | Asks autoplay for a song first, so Next always does something. On day 1, autoplay once came back with nothing after a song (Galactic Boss Battle), so the queue ended there and Next did nothing. Now another song of the same genre fills in, and `coda.log` says `autoplay: the engine had nothing`. Taking songs out of the queue tops it back up. |
| **New queue from here** | Menu on Now playing (or on the playing song in the Queue): what was queued after it goes, and autoplay carries on from this song, as if you'd just picked it. |
| **Fresh finds, Deep cuts** | The list is the whole pool now (it showed 12 while the header said 96), with Play all and Shuffle. At every visit what you've played or disliked since drops out, so they move on as you listen. The PC side now gives them 400 / 300 to work from, starting with the next card sync. |

## Playback that stops

On day 1 a song stuttered and stopped and nothing played again until a restart. The
Bluetooth log shows the stream stopping about two minutes in, with no error. A watchdog now
catches it: if the player says "playing" and the song's clock hasn't moved for 6 seconds,
`coda.log` gets a line about the audio path (`os: stuck ... audio, pcm, mixer, buffered,
bt stream ...`) and the player tries, in order: start the queue again where it was; restart
Bluetooth and play again once the earbuds are back; restart the player (it comes back
paused, the queue kept). The next time it happens, the log says where it stuck.

## Earbuds

- **Taking them out pauses, putting them back plays.** From where the Galaxy Buds say each
  bud sits; one back in within 30 minutes and it plays again. Only the right bud was
  connected all day on day 1.
- **Battery:** the right bud went down about 20% an hour while streaming (Samsung rate the
  Buds3 Pro at 6 to 7 hours, 14 to 17% an hour). `bt.log` now says which packets the link
  uses as the audio comes on. If it's basic rate (339 bytes) rather than EDR (679/1021),
  both radios are on air two to three times as long, and that's the next thing to fix.
- **The chip not answering at power-on** (4 boots of about 25): the retries now hold it off
  longer and start the UART afresh.

## Charging and the battery figure

- **The charger no longer starts the player.** It still wakes it (the power chip does
  that), but Coda shows a charge screen: the cell, the %, "Charging" in amber or "Charged"
  in green. Any button starts the player proper, and unplugging switches it off again, as
  does the screen going dark. Bluetooth waits, so a player charging at night doesn't take
  the earbuds from the phone.
- **Amber while charging, green when full**, on every theme's battery; a bolt on Sakurai's.
- **The status light** is handed to the charger (power chip CHGLED, lit while charging).
  Whether the H2's light is wired to it we'll know when you plug it in. `coda.log`
  records what the register held before.
- **Which charger:** any USB charger. The H2 now draws up to 780 mA from a wall charger (what
  the original firmware used; Rockbox capped it at 500), so a 1 A or bigger one is plenty.
  A fast charger isn't faster. A full charge is about 2.5 to 3 hours with the player off.
- **The % no longer jumps up after a rest** (off at 30%, on later at 46%). The power chip's
  gauge has no model of this battery and reads high after a rest, then falls faster. Coda
  now shows it scaled to carry on from the last figure: on battery it only goes down and
  reaches 0 when the gauge does; on the charger it follows the gauge. `battery.csv` keeps
  both (the level column is the gauge, the new last column what Coda shows).
- **Bluetooth costs battery:** playing over the earbuds with the screen off ran at 12%/h on
  the gauge; on wired it was about 7%/h. The CPU was only 11% busy, so the difference is in
  the radio path, not the animations.

## Screen and themes

- **No flash at power-on:** the brightness is kept in the power chip too, so the screen
  comes on at your setting instead of full brightness until the card's settings are read.
- **Day and night by the sun:** the auto themes (Diorama, Room, Sakurai auto) go dark at
  dusk and light at dawn for today's date. They no longer switch at 7:30 pm all year.
  Location: New York by default (`sun=` in `/.coda/settings.txt`, hundredths of a degree).
  The card was on "Diorama night" (always dark); it's on "Diorama" (auto) now.
- **Diorama:** the shelf's things are redrawn at the photo's scale with the detail the
  posters have: the aloe and the succulent in their pots, both piranha plants, the silver
  frame, the green train, the clock, Luigi, the block figures, a fuller fern. The case is
  the Fractal Terra now (lattice front and its blue light, the walnut foot, the power
  button). The earbuds' case moved to the free end of the desk by the mug, and Bubloo's
  nap spot moved over a little to make room.

## Evening: the car, Hollow Knight, a card that filled up

- **The song on the car's display** (a 2013 Corolla showed only "Connected"): the
  Bluetooth stack refused the car's "what's playing?" requests. It answers them now, and
  tells the car when the track, play/pause or position changes. Patch 0019, see
  [BLUETOOTH.md](BLUETOOTH.md). Firmware 91c1bef5, on the card at 21:14.
- **Hollow Knight and Silksong:** most of both were in the library already from other
  uploads, untagged, so neither showed up as a soundtrack. They're three albums now:
  *Hollow Knight* (26), *Hollow Knight: Gods & Nightmares* (15, the DLC album) and
  *Hollow Knight: Silksong* (53), with Christopher Larkin as the artist; 10 missing tracks
  were downloaded (`scripts/harvest_ost_playlists.py`, catalog in
  `scripts/curate_osts_2026_10_15.py`).
- **Soundtrack songs that were already in the library** got their album tags this
  afternoon, but the library index skips files it has read before, so 143 of them (plus
  Hollow Knight's and Silksong's) still had no album there and never counted as part of
  their soundtrack. `--retag` now updates the index too (295 songs).
- **The card filled up mid-sync.** It's a 64 GB card; the export reckoned it had 90 GB to
  work with, because it counted every song its list (`/.coda/music.tsv`) ever named,
  including ones removed since or never copied. 518 of 8,693 songs were copied and the
  H2's index listed songs that weren't there. `export_h2.py` now counts only songs on the
  card, and if a copy ever stops short it rebuilds the index from the songs that made it.
  The next sync chose 17,862 songs (~60.8 GB): every song from the YouTube Music history
  and every whole soundtrack first, older "sounds like" picks taken off to make room.
- **Plays from the H2:** the 441 play/skip/like events since 10-11 are in the PC's history
  (the sync brought them back after a test run's copy was wrongly removed earlier).

## Late evening: autoplay is the radio engine again

- **Why:** the H2's own log. After a skip, the older walk picked a song that sounded like the
  one you'd skipped, and you skipped that too 53% of the time (12% after a finish). On
  10-14 an OmarCameUp start ran into 21 Savage, Trippie Redd and Juice WRLD, artists you've
  played 0–2 times, until you left by hand.
- **Now:** autoplay is the radio for the song you started: what goes with it, mostly from
  what people play together, plus songs you already play. Skips change it:
  - any skip short of the end counts against the direction;
  - three in a row, or skipping the song you started with nothing kept since, moves it to
    one of your most played lately that sounds unlike what you skipped ("Changing it up:
    on the radio for ...");
  - a collaboration counts for each artist on it, so one artist can't fill the queue;
  - one version of a song per sitting.
  Details and numbers in [docs/RADIO_ENGINE.md](../RADIO_ENGINE.md). Firmware 3de635d0 plus
  `radio.bin` went onto the card at 22:39; every sync builds `radio.bin` from now on.
