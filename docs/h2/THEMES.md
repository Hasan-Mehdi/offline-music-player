# Coda's themes on the H2

Settings > Theme picks one; the choice is `skin=` in `/.coda/settings.txt`, and its power-on
animation plays from the next boot (it's read before the library loads).

## How it's built

- `firmware/ui/app.c` draws every view through a theme (`skin` table): page, header, list row
  (and the cursor over it), label, button, now bar, home, Now playing (optional), mosaic
  panels (Library, Settings), Explore's shelves, Search's keys, the sheet, and a palette
  for the screens every theme shares (the Wheel, Now playing, the clock).
- The themes are `th_poster.h` (Sakurai), `th_pixel.h` (the pixel look), `th_room.h`
  (Room, Bubloo, the door), `th_jtd.h` (Jukebox, Toybox, Deck), `th_crd.h` (Crate, Receiver, Diorama) and
  `th_anim.h` (power on / off) -- included into app.c, so they see its internals.
- Shared parts: `sym.c` (28 geometric symbols with focus flourishes), `pix.c` (the 2x2 grid,
  stepped corners, the room's materials, symbols snapped to the grid, the pointer),
  `pixfont.h` (built-in fonts, generated by `firmware/tools/gen_pixfonts.py`: Archivo Black
  11, Pixelify Sans 11 -- pixel-exact only at 11 px --, Silkscreen 8 and 16).
- Motion: after any input the app draws ~30 fps for 0.75 s (the pop, the flourish, the
  cursor sliding, the sheet rising), then goes back to idle redraws.
- Song titles, artists and times always use the card's fonts (CJK fallback); pixel labels
  that can't be spelled in the built-in fonts fall back to them too.
- Mockups: `docs/h2/menu`, `inner` (Sakurai), `room`, `pixel` (the reference renders,
  `firmware/tools/mock_*.py`). On-device renders: `firmware/host/coda_shots.c` writes
  `t<theme>_*.png` and boot/off frames for every theme.

## Bubloo (Room)

- Sprites on the 2x grid in `th_room.h` (`CAT_BODY`, `CAT_HEAD`, `CAT_PAWS`; the eyes are
  worked out per frame), drawn from the listener's photos of him: a long-haired brown tabby,
  white bib, muzzle and mitts, green eyes, a big dark tail. The tail is a chain of discs that
  hangs over the ledge and sways (`cat_tail`).
- What he does comes from the clock and the player, never from frame-to-frame state
  (`pet_pose`): awake while music plays -- content, eyes half shut now and then, the tail
  swinging harder with the bass (`plat_spectrum`) -- blinking, flicking an ear, curling the
  tail tip; a button wakes him and he looks at the panel you're on; 14 s without music he
  dozes, 30 s he's asleep (head down, z's), and wakes with a yawn.
- He's redrawn on his own at 12 fps between full frames through the hub path
  (`app_draw_hub` -> `pet_frame`): his corner of the screen is kept from the last full frame
  (`pet_keep`, at most 128 x 112), he's drawn over it and only that rectangle goes to the
  LCD. Only on Room's home and Now playing, with no sheet up, and only while the screen is
  lit.
- `SHOTS_ROOM=1 coda_shots ...` renders just Room: the home, his frames, Now playing awake /
  dozing / asleep, and the door's frames (`r_*.png`).
