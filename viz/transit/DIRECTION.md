# Film direction — "Night Service" (one continuous ride)

One committed direction (per the anti-AI-slop rule: decide once, stay consistent).

**v2 note.** v1 ("Wayfinding") was a transit-skinned copy of viz/film's structure —
chapter slides over a god-view map. Rejected for exactly that. v2 keeps the print
language but changes the STRUCTURE: no chapters, no atlas. The whole film is one
unbroken ride.

**The line:** it is 3:07 AM and the player is driving. You board at one lo-fi song
and the entire film is a single continuous camera move that follows the player's
actual autoplay engine (1.0·sound + 0.3·co-play − artist cooldown(6), ε 0.20) from
stop to stop — 17 stops, a real clip playing at every one, crossfading like a set.
Everything that would have been a "scene" happens en route: genre districts are
neighbourhoods you cross (banner + name printed on the map), text→music search is a
mid-ride REQUEST STOP that reroutes the bus across the city, the duplicate upload is
something you pass, and the only overview is the final continuous pull-up that
reveals the whole route. Ends in an explorable map.

**Materials**
- Ground: mid-tone paper `#e5e1d5` (v1's near-white read glaring), ink `#191813`,
  hairline rules + crop marks. Muted district inks — riso saturation, no purple.
- Accents: IKB `#002fa7` = request/planned route; signal red `#d62b1a` = the ride.
  Dashed segments = ε-exploration and request hops.
- Type: Archivo Black slugs (sparse — open + terminus only); Consolas mono
  everywhere else; Solari split-flap boards (station, request, now-strip).
- Motion: ONE camera, kernel-smoothed over the stop sequence (no keyframe cuts),
  zoom breathes out on long hops. Cards/banners enter mechanically and leave quietly.
- Sound: wall-to-wall — 17 real clips crossfading, flap ticks, arrival dings,
  departure puffs, reroute bell, slug thumps. All mechanical, no synth pads.

**Avoids every named tell** (no dark+neon, glow, glassmorphism, scanlines, HUD,
techy fonts, sidebar shell) and avoids v1's tell: same-structure-different-skin.
